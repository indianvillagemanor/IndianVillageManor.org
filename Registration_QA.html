Registration Walkthrough

Welcome! We are testing our new registration process. Please follow the steps below to create your account and then share your feedback in the section below.

  1. Visit the website: Scan the QR code above or type https://indianvillagemanor.org into your web browser.
  2. Find the Registration Page: Locate and click the "Register" or "Create an account" link from the homepage navigation menu.
  3. Fill out the form: Provide your details including Name, Email, Phone, and Unit Number. Make sure to indicate whether you are a Resident, an Owner, or both.
  4. Submit your information: After submitting, you should see a "Verification required" message. Your account will be in a pending state until an administrator reviews and approves it.
  5. Wait for Verification: Once verified by our team, you will receive an email. Going forward, you can securely log in using a "Magic Link" sent directly to your inbox.
